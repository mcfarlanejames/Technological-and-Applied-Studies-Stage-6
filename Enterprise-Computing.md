# Enterprise Computing

## Overview
Enterprise Computing encompasses the large-scale IT systems and infrastructure used by organisations to manage business operations, data, and communications. It focuses on scalability, reliability, integration, and strategic technology alignment with business objectives.

## Learning Objectives
By studying Enterprise Computing, students will:
- Understand enterprise IT architecture and systems
- Learn database management and data handling
- Master networking and connectivity solutions
- Develop cybersecurity and data protection skills
- Apply enterprise integration and business intelligence

## Core Topics

### 1. Enterprise Systems Architecture
- Enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems
- System integration and middleware
- Service-oriented architecture (SOA)
- Microservices and containerisation
- Cloud computing platforms
- Scalability and performance optimisation

### 2. Database Management
- Relational databases and SQL
- Data modelling and schema design
- Database administration and maintenance
- Backup, recovery, and disaster planning
- Data warehousing and data lakes
- NoSQL and non-relational databases

### 3. Networking and Infrastructure
- Local area networks (LAN) and wide area networks (WAN)
- Internet protocols and IP addressing
- Routing and switching technologies
- Virtual private networks (VPN)
- Network security and firewalls
- Cloud infrastructure and virtualisation

### 4. Cybersecurity and Data Protection
- Information security principles and policies
- Access control and authentication
- Encryption and data protection
- Threat detection and prevention
- Compliance and regulatory requirements
- Incident response and recovery

### 5. Business Intelligence and Analytics
- Data collection and integration
- Data analysis and reporting
- Business analytics and dashboards
- Predictive modelling and forecasting
- Data visualisation
- Decision support systems

### 6. Enterprise Applications
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Supply Chain Management (SCM)
- Human Resource Management (HRM)
- Financial management systems
- Business process management (BPM)
- Workflow automation

### 7. IT Service Management
- IT operations and service delivery
- Incident and problem management
- Change management procedures
- Configuration management
- Service level agreements (SLAs)
- IT governance and compliance

### 8. Cloud and Emerging Technologies
- Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)
- Platform as a Service (PaaS)
- Software as a Service (SaaS)
- Cloud migration and integration
- Artificial intelligence in enterprise systems
- Blockchain and distributed ledgers
