# Food Technology

## Overview
Food Technology is the practical application of science and engineering principles to food production, processing, preservation, and distribution. It combines chemistry, microbiology, engineering, and nutrition to create safe, quality food products.

## Learning Objectives
By studying Food Technology, students will:
- Understand food science principles
- Learn food safety and hygiene standards
- Master food processing techniques
- Develop product development skills
- Apply sustainability in food systems

## Core Topics

### 1. Food Science Fundamentals
- Food composition (proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, minerals)
- Chemical reactions in food preparation
- Enzymatic processes and fermentation
- Maillard reaction and caramelisation
- pH and acidity effects on food

### 2. Food Microbiology and Safety
- Pathogenic microorganisms and food-borne illnesses
- HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points)
- Sanitation and hygiene practices
- Food storage and preservation methods
- Contamination prevention and control
- Testing and quality assurance

### 3. Food Processing and Preservation
- Thermal processing (pasteurisation, sterilisation)
- Non-thermal preservation (freezing, drying, irradiation)
- Canning and bottling techniques
- Fermentation processes
- Modified atmosphere packaging (MAP)
- Emerging technologies (high-pressure processing, pulsed electric fields)

### 4. Food Product Development
- Sensory evaluation and testing
- Recipe formulation and scaling
- Ingredient functionality and interactions
- Product shelf-life and stability testing
- Consumer acceptance testing
- Regulatory compliance and labelling

### 5. Nutrition and Health
- Macronutrient and micronutrient composition
- Nutritional balance and dietary guidelines
- Functional foods and supplements
- Allergies and dietary restrictions
- Menu planning and nutritional analysis
- Health claims and marketing

### 6. Equipment and Machinery
- Food processing equipment (mixers, grinders, separators)
- Heat exchangers and thermal processing
- Refrigeration and freezing systems
- Packaging machinery
- Monitoring and control systems
- Maintenance and sanitation

### 7. Sustainability in Food Production
- Sustainable agriculture practices
- Food waste reduction and management
- Packaging sustainability and alternatives
- Energy efficiency in processing
- Water conservation
- Local and seasonal food production

### 8. Food Regulations and Standards
- Food safety regulations (FDA, FSANZ, EU standards)
- Labelling requirements
- Allergen declarations
- Certification systems (ISO 22000, BRC)
- Traceability and recall procedures
- Import/export regulations
