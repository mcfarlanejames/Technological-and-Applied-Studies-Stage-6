# Industrial Technology

## Overview
Industrial Technology focuses on the practical application of technology in manufacturing, production, and industrial operations. It encompasses automation, process optimization, maintenance, and modern manufacturing techniques.

## Learning Objectives
By studying Industrial Technology, students will:
- Understand industrial systems and processes
- Learn automation and control principles
- Master maintenance and troubleshooting
- Develop skills in production management
- Apply lean and sustainable manufacturing practices

## Core Topics

### 1. Manufacturing Processes
- Machining and cutting operations
- Forming and shaping techniques (pressing, forging, casting)
- Joining processes (welding, riveting, adhesives)
- Finishing and surface treatment
- Additive manufacturing (3D printing)
- Quality control in production

### 2. Industrial Automation
- Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs)
- Robotics and robotic systems
- Conveyor and material handling systems
- Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machines
- SCADA systems and monitoring
- Human-machine interfaces (HMI)

### 3. Process Control Systems
- Open-loop and closed-loop control
- PID control systems
- Sensors and transducers
- Actuators and motors
- Control valve operations
- Feedback and calibration

### 4. Maintenance and Operations
- Preventive maintenance schedules
- Predictive maintenance techniques
- Condition monitoring
- Troubleshooting and diagnostics
- Safety lockout/tagout procedures
- Maintenance documentation and logs

### 5. Lean Manufacturing
- Waste elimination (8 types of waste)
- Just-In-Time (JIT) production
- Continuous improvement (Kaizen)
- 5S methodology (Sort, Set, Shine, Standardize, Sustain)
- Value stream mapping
- Six Sigma and quality control

### 6. Supply Chain and Logistics
- Inventory management
- Production scheduling
- Demand forecasting
- Warehouse operations
- Distribution and logistics
- Supply chain optimization

### 7. Industrial Safety and Environment
- OSHA standards and compliance
- Hazard identification and assessment
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)
- Emergency procedures and response
- Environmental regulations
- Sustainability in manufacturing

### 8. Industrial Technology Systems
- Industrial IoT (IIoT) and Industry 4.0
- Data collection and analytics
- Cloud-based monitoring systems
- Cybersecurity for industrial systems
- Digital transformation in manufacturing
- Predictive analytics
