# Engineering Studies

## Overview
Engineering Studies encompasses the application of scientific and mathematical principles to design, build, and improve structures, machines, systems, and processes. It covers multiple disciplines and emphasises problem-solving, innovation, and real-world application.

## Learning Objectives
By studying Engineering Studies, students will:
- Understand fundamental engineering principles
- Learn design and problem-solving methodologies
- Master technical analysis and calculations
- Develop project management skills
- Apply ethics and sustainability in engineering

## Core Topics

### 1. Mechanical Engineering
- Force, motion, and mechanics
- Thermodynamics and heat transfer
- Fluid mechanics and hydraulics
- Machine design and analysis
- Structural analysis
- Vibration and dynamics
- Manufacturing processes

### 2. Civil Engineering
- Structural design and analysis
- Foundation design and geotechnics
- Transportation and highway engineering
- Water resources and hydraulics
- Surveying and mapping
- Environmental engineering
- Construction management

### 3. Electrical Engineering
- Circuit analysis and design
- AC and DC circuits
- Electromagnetics
- Power systems and distribution
- Control systems
- Signal processing
- Electronics and semiconductor devices

### 4. Chemical Engineering
- Process design and simulation
- Thermodynamics and kinetics
- Mass and heat transfer
- Fluid flow in pipes and equipment
- Reactors and chemical processes
- Environmental and safety considerations
- Plant design and optimisation

### 5. Materials Science and Engineering
- Material properties and classification
- Metals and alloys
- Polymers and composites
- Ceramics and glasses
- Material selection and design
- Corrosion and degradation
- Testing and characterisation

### 6. Engineering Design Process
- Problem definition and analysis
- Brainstorming and ideation
- Conceptual design
- Detailed design
- Prototyping and testing
- Refinement and optimisation
- Documentation and specifications

### 7. Technical Mathematics and Analysis
- Calculus and differential equations
- Linear algebra and matrices
- Numerical methods
- Statistical analysis
- Computational modelling
- Finite element analysis (FEA)
- Simulation and prediction

### 8. Engineering Graphics and CAD
- Technical drawing and conventions
- Orthographic projection
- Isometric and perspective views
- Computer-aided design (CAD) software
- 3D modelling
- Documentation and blueprints
- Design visualisation

### 9. Project Management and Teamwork
- Project planning and scheduling
- Resource allocation
- Cost estimation and budgeting
- Risk management
- Quality assurance
- Documentation and communication
- Leadership and collaboration

### 10. Engineering Ethics and Sustainability
- Professional ethics and responsibility
- Safety and risk assessment
- Environmental impact assessment
- Sustainable design practices
- Green engineering
- Social responsibility
- Legal and regulatory compliance
