# Design and Technology

## Overview
Design and Technology integrates creative thinking with practical problem-solving, combining aesthetics, functionality, and innovation. It encompasses product design, user experience, CAD, prototyping, and the iterative process of bringing ideas to life.

## Learning Objectives
By studying Design and Technology, students will:
- Understand design thinking and methodology
- Learn CAD and digital design tools
- Master prototyping and testing techniques
- Develop skills in user-centred design
- Apply sustainability and ethical design principles

## Core Topics

### 1. Design Thinking and Process
- Problem identification and research
- Ideation and brainstorming techniques
- Concept development and evaluation
- Iterative design process
- User research and feedback
- Design constraints and requirements

### 2. Product Design
- Form and function relationships
- Ergonomics and human factors
- Material selection and properties
- Manufacturability and cost analysis
- Safety and risk assessment
- Aesthetic principles and styling

### 3. User Experience (UX) Design
- User research and personas
- Wireframing and prototyping
- Information architecture
- Usability testing and evaluation
- Accessibility standards (WCAG)
- Inclusive design principles

### 4. Computer-Aided Design (CAD)
- 2D and 3D modelling techniques
- Technical drawing standards
- Assembly and simulation
- Rendering and visualisation
- Design libraries and parametric modelling
- CAD software (AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360)

### 5. Prototyping and Testing
- Rapid prototyping techniques
- 3D printing and additive manufacturing
- Scale models and mock-ups
- Functional testing and validation
- Stress testing and failure analysis
- Iterative improvements

### 6. Sustainable and Ethical Design
- Lifecycle assessment (LCA)
- Material sustainability and recycling
- Energy efficiency in design
- Ethical considerations and social impact
- Circular economy principles
- Environmental regulations and standards

### 7. Design Communication
- Technical drawings and specifications
- Design portfolios and presentations
- Visual communication and graphics
- Documentation and manuals
- Design briefs and proposals
- Stakeholder communication

## Real-World Applications
- Consumer product design
- Industrial design
- Furniture and interior design
- Packaging design
- Graphic and digital design
- UI/UX for software and apps
- Automotive and aerospace design
- Sustainable product development

## Key Skills
- Creative thinking and innovation
- Problem-solving and critical analysis
- Technical proficiency with design tools
- Communication and presentation skills
- Teamwork and collaboration
- User empathy and research abilities
- Attention to detail and quality
- Project management

## Design Tools and Software
- CAD: AutoCAD, SolidWorks, Fusion 360, Blender
- 3D Printing: PreForm, Cura, Fusion 360
- Graphic Design: Adobe Creative Suite, Figma
- Prototyping: Adobe XD, Sketch, Framer
- Analysis: ANSYS, Fusion 360 Simulation

## Design Methodologies
- Design Thinking (Empathise, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test)
- Lean Design
- Agile Design
- Human-Centred Design
- Service Design
- Systems Thinking

## Career Paths
- Product designer
- UX/UI designer
- Industrial designer
- CAD technician
- Design engineer
- Graphic designer

- Innovation consultant

## Design Principles
- Form follows function
- Simplicity and clarity
- Consistency and hierarchy
- Feedback and responsiveness
- Accessibility and inclusivity
- Sustainability and ethics
- Aesthetic excellence
